Meet Our Master Chefs

Culinary artists dedicated to preserving authentic Indian flavors

Chef Rajesh Kumar

Executive Chef

With over 20 years of experience in North Indian cuisine, Chef Rajesh specializes in traditional Mughlai dishes and tandoori specialties. He trained under master chefs in Delhi and brings authentic flavors to every plate.

Chef Priya Sharma

Sous Chef

Expert in South Indian cuisine, Chef Priya creates authentic dosas, idlis, and coastal specialties. Her passion for vegetarian cooking brings innovative plant-based options to our menu.

Chef Amit Patel

Pastry Chef

Specializing in traditional Indian sweets and desserts, Chef Amit creates authentic mithai using age-old recipes passed down through generations.
